This folder contains my project over a ten-week internship with Visualize Risk, LLC.

1. Automated Data Pipelines & Customer Lifecycle System: Built ten Power Automate cloud flows to automatically ingest lead data from Microsoft Marketplace and SharePoint into Dataverse, process customer and contact records, and manage entitlement lifecycles (trials, active licenses, expirations).

2. Lifecycle Email Orchestration: Designed multi-stage email sequences triggered by lifecycle states, ensuring commercial email compliance with standardized automatic opt-out.

3. Reporting: Authored comprehensive custom Power BI dashboards analyzing conversion, retention, customer engagement, geography, product version adoption, and revenue estimates. 

4. Documentation: Authored system documentation, including video walkthroughs.

Dataverse is used for data, Power Automate executes actions, Outlook is where the communication takes place, and Power BI is used for reporting.

Over the course of this internship, I learned firsthand how to turn complex business needs into end-to-end automated solutions across the Microsoft Power Platform, Dataverse, Power BI, and Google Analytics. Gaining a true 360-degree view of the business showed me how technical architecture directly drives customer retention, compliance, and growth.
